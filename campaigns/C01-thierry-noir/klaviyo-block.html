Featured artist
The Man Who
Painted the Wall
Thierry Noir is a French artist, born in 1958, who lives and works in Berlin. Regarded as the first artist to paint the Berlin Wall, he covered almost 5 km of it during the 1980s with his bright, cartoon-like heads. Surviving originals can be seen on Wall fragments and at the East Side Gallery, while his iconic style is considered a foundation of the street-art movement.
