FROM THE STUDIO TO THE GALLERY

In late August, Theo Bardsley arrived at TUMO Gallery for an artist residency. Visitors stepped inside his working studio in early September to see his process up close.

Today, that chapter continues with Echoes of the Room, on view in Vilnius until 11 October.

MEET THEO BARDSLEY

Born in 1997 and based in London, Bardsley is a British figurative painter. A self-taught artist who studied History of Art at the University of Manchester, he turns everyday encounters into richly textured scenes of intimacy and memory.

WHAT STAYS AFTER WE LEAVE?

Drawing on experiences in London, Morocco and Vilnius, the exhibition explores the boundary between private life and public space. A figure, a shadow or an empty room can hold the feeling of someone who has just left.
