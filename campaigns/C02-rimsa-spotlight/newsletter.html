Jonas Rimša's The Light of Pachamama is a unique original in oil on canvas.
View the work
One painting.
Three reasons it quietly endures.
The Light of Pachamama depicts a mystical Aymara ritual shaped by light, shadow and collective presence. Its sculptural brushwork and vibrating palette reward close looking, revealing the inner force and archaic dignity of the Andean spirit.
View the work
Visit the gallery
Within the Painting
 
Look closer at the elements that give this ritual scene its spiritual gravity.
Sacred Light
A glowing sacred vessel or fire altar forms the spiritual axis around which the figures gather.
Sacred Circle
Women in red monteras and monumental male silhouettes form a closed circle, expressing a sacred bond with Pachamama.
Sculptural Brushwork
Expressive, sculptural brushstrokes convey the inner force of the Andean spirit rather than its outward appearance.
