ONE PAINTING. THREE REASONS IT QUIETLY ENDURES.

The Light of Pachamama depicts a mystical Aymara ritual shaped by light, shadow
and collective presence. Its sculptural brushwork and vibrating palette reward
close looking, revealing the inner force and archaic dignity of the Andean spirit.

View the work: https://tumogalerija.lt/products/jonas-rimsa-pachamama-1
Visit the gallery: https://tumogalerija.lt/pages/kontaktai

---

WITHIN THE PAINTING

Look closer at the elements that give this ritual scene its spiritual gravity.

- Sacred Light: A glowing sacred vessel or fire altar forms the spiritual axis
  around which the figures gather.
- Sacred Circle: Women in red monteras and monumental male silhouettes form a
  closed circle, expressing a sacred bond with Pachamama.
- Sculptural Brushwork: Expressive, sculptural brushstrokes convey the inner
  force of the Andean spirit rather than its outward appearance.

View the work: https://tumogalerija.lt/products/jonas-rimsa-pachamama-1
